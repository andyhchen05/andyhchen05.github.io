Chess vs AI

A from-scratch chess engine, ported to Python from Gary Linscott's GarboChess-JS, running entirely in your browser via WebAssembly (Pyodide) — no server involved. Legal move generation, alpha-beta search with iterative deepening and a quiescence search, and a classic material + piece-square-table evaluation. Click a piece, then click a highlighted square to move.
